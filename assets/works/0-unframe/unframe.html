un|frame - brand identity & studio positioning

Category Brand Identity
Client unframe — Event Strategy Studio
Year 2026
Location Tbilisi, Georgia

Every event agency in this market runs the same playbook: mood boards, themes, decor. unframe was built on a different premise — that an event without a strategic argument is just decoration.

The identity had to carry that argument before a single space was designed.

The system had to hold up outside a style guide — inside a client-facing pitch deck, on Instagram, on a printed booklet.

Three phases structure every engagement: Understand, Argue, Build.

The client approves the argument before a single element is designed.

the argument doesn't stop at the print booklet — it holds even on a wall of raw concrete.
the same rule, three colors, one rationed red, held across a feed instead of a page.
